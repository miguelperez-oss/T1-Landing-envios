12. FAQ (#faq)
────────────────────────────────────────

H2: Frequently Asked Questions

Description: Transparent, direct, and reliable — just like our platform.
             Quick answers about shipments, rates, and more.

Q: What is T1 Shipping and how does it work?
A: T1 Shipping is a platform to quote, compare, and ship from one place
   with leading carriers and competitive rates. Create your account in
   minutes and manage your entire operation: labels, pickups, tracking,
   incidents, and reports.

Q: Do I need an online store to use T1 Shipping?
A: No. You can use T1 Shipping even if you sell from a physical store,
   via WhatsApp, Instagram, or marketplaces. If you already have an
   online store, you can integrate it to centralize all your shipments.

Q: Which carriers can I ship with?
A: T1 Shipping connects with leading carriers so you can compare rates
   and delivery times from the same dashboard: UPS, DHL, FedEx,
   99minutos, and ampm.

Q: What information do I need to get a quote?
A: You just need: origin ZIP code, destination ZIP code, package weight
   and dimensions.

Q: How do I pay for my labels?
A: It works with prepaid balance. You add funds to your account via
   SPEI transfer or credit/debit card. Your balance never expires and
   is deducted as you purchase labels.

Q: Where do I drop off my packages?
A: It depends on the service and carrier. You can drop off your package
   at the nearest branch or, if available, schedule a home pickup
   with the carrier.

Q: Can I get invoices for my shipments?
A: Yes. You can request your invoice from your account using your
   tax information.
